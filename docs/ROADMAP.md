# Outlaw — build log and roadmap

The game is a single HTML page built from `src/` by `node tools/build.mjs pages/outlaw.html dist/outlaw.html`.
Each step was published as an artifact and screenshot-checked in headless Chromium
(`tools/shot.mjs`, `tools/interiors.mjs`, `tools/simtest.mjs`).

## Done

| 19 | war | War with the Marcher Lords of Varn: border raids build tension; an emergency council votes for war or a bought peace; the crown musters hosts and levies men everywhere; in Ashford a recruiting sergeant spends a day in the square and marches off with real villagers (families grieve, letters bring news of the fallen, survivors come home after the peace, some wounded); armies seek each other by road, join up, fight abstract non-graphic battles, fall back, burn places and cut roads; refugees settle in empty houses; war taxes and the crown's levy on the parish chest; grain requisitions; crown orders for the smiths; the King's host marches down the King's Road behind its banner; take the King's shilling and fight a campaign (or desert, a crime); armies, battles and the border shown on the map; treaties that remember who won |
| 18 | honest work | Ask a master at their workplace for a day's work; hour-long shifts at the trade's workstations pass time, tire you, really produce goods and pay you from the till (fractions of a penny accrue); 16 trade skills learned by working (novice → apprentice → journeyman → master) with better pay as you improve; after several good days the master offers an apprenticeship (steady work, double learning); masters refuse known thieves and the disliked; shop prices follow your merchant and local standing and the shopkeeper's opinion, a bad name costs extra and some refuse to serve you; people who've heard of your public deeds greet you by them; skills, employer and honest earnings shown in the satchel; bard sprite with a lute in every direction |
| 17 | history, news, legacy | The Chronicle (C): a world timeline of facts sorted into rulers, politics, roads, markets, harvests, disasters, trades, gangs, crimes, deaths and people, across every place; facts stored apart from beliefs — each NPC keeps what they've heard and from whom; the same event told differently by eyewitnesses, the crier (the council's line), the watch, wandering bards (ballads that inflate and mythologise, sung in the tavern on Freyday and Saturnday nights), the Kingsbridge broadsheet (sensational), travelling merchants (muddled places) and rumour over a jug (names swapped, numbers drift); Hearsay tab sets each version beside the record; Your story records the player's deeds, including those only you know; notable dead get memorial stones in the churchyard with epitaphs and portraits on their family's wall; children speak of dead parents; trades remember their founders |
| 16 | cities & castles | Kingsbridge, a walled capital on a river: curtain walls with towers and gatehouses, two bridges, a grand market of stalls, Guildhall, cathedral, and districts (noble quarter with mansions, townhouses, jeweller, apothecary and cathedral school; workshops with armourer, carpenter, butcher; riverside warehouse; tenements in the poor quarter; hospital); Thornbury Castle with keep, bailey, barracks, chapel, armoury, great hall and a village outside the gate; interiors for every new building type; tenements house several families; building security sets lock difficulty and jewellers keep a night watchman who hears the pick; city economy with river-barge imports, quay exports, dockers hired daily, guild dues, several shops per trade sharing custom |
