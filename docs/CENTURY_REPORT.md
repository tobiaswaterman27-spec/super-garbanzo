# The century run: 66 years of Eldoria

The run stepped the whole realm, hour by hour, from a new game. It reached year 66 before the cloud machine restarted and stopped it. Ashford was the village simulated in full; the rest of the realm was simulated in the abstract.

## People

- 507 births, 193 deaths, 88 weddings.
- 40 households emigrated.
- The oldest person was 86.
- Average age at death was 39 (median 61), because 77 of the deaths were children under five.

| Cause of death | Deaths |
|---|---|
| Old age | 103 |
| Illness in childhood (fever, spotted fever, flux, cough) | 77 |
| Accidents at work (a fall from scaffolding, a falling tree, the mill-wheel and others) | 8 |
| Childbirth (the baby lived each time) | 4 |
| Beheaded | 1 |

## Crowns

Five monarchs reigned:

- King Godric II died in year 0.
- King Stephen died aged 27, after 11 years.
- Queen Agnes was crowned aged 11 under a regent, and reigned 46 years.
- Queen Alys reigned only 2 years.
- Queen Alys II was crowned aged 4, with a regent.

## Conflict

- 10 rebellions (Frostmere, then Eastmarch, then Westcliff), all crushed and their leaders exiled.
- 28 wars with the northern enemy, each ending in a peace; 38 times levied men walked home.
- 164 feuds between lords.

## Disasters and events

- 124 river floods and 140 fires.
- 317 failed harvests across the realm.
- 5 plague outbreaks, which spread to 13 more places.
- 20 roads closed by storms and 15 caravans robbed.
- 332 lords died and their land passed to an heir.

## Economy

- 30 businesses; 24 were still open at the end (21 closed and 7 opened over the years).
- Prices stayed steady: bread ₳2 to ₳5.5, ale ₳1 to ₳1.5, wheat ₳2 to ₳4, meat ₳3 to ₳6.
- The village chest held at about ₳9,000 to ₳11,000, with 524 rounds of public works paid to the poorest.
- Across the realm, iron fell from ₳8 to ₳4, while cloth rose from ₳10 to about ₳22.

## Crime

229 crimes, every one a burglary. Nobody was ever in the gaol at a yearly check, and the one gang had no members after year 0.

## Weak spots the run showed

- Jobs didn't keep up with people: at year 50 only 76 of 170 adults had work, because the village grew but kept its 30 businesses.
- Crime was one-note: only burglaries, with no arrests and a gang that died out.
- Floods and fires were still frequent, about two each a year.
