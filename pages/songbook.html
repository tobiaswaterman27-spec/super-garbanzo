The Outlaw Songbook

Every song and tune in Outlaw, played the way the town plays them: lute, harp, recorder, fiddle, shawm, hurdy-gurdy, tabor, bells and voices, all made in code as you listen. The songs are sung to their own tunes, fitted to the words syllable by syllable.
