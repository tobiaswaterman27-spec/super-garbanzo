Every frame is generated from the same skeleton: skin, sleeves, trousers and boots are painted onto one set of limb shapes, so clothing can't leave gaps when arms and legs move. Lighting comes from the upper left on every sprite in the world.

Townsfolk

NPCs are assembled from shared parts: body by age, height and build genes; faces from eye, brow, nose and jaw genes; outfits from their trade and wealth. None of these are hand-placed.

Bloodlines

Children inherit skin tone, hair colour and type, eye colour, height, build and facial structure from both parents, with small mutations and an occasional throwback to an older generation. Siblings resemble each other without being copies.

Sprite sheet

The full sheet for your character: 12 animations × 4 directions. Frames are 32×48 and generated on demand, then kept in a bounded cache so only nearby people hold pixel data.
